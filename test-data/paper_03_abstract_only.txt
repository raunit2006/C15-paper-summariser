Title: Noise-Robust Keyword Spotting for Low-Power Wearables

Abstract
Keyword spotting models deployed on wearable devices must run on
microcontrollers with under 256KB of RAM while staying robust to wind noise,
fabric rubbing, and motion artifacts absent from typical training data. We
train a 40KB quantized CNN on a mixture of clean speech and device-collected
noise recordings and show it keeps false-reject rate under 5% in windy
outdoor conditions where an unmodified baseline model exceeds 30%.
