Title: FastRoute: Reducing Delivery Times with Reinforcement-Learned Routing

Abstract
We present FastRoute, a reinforcement-learning-based delivery routing system
that reduces average delivery time by 40% compared to a standard
shortest-path baseline, evaluated on a simulated city of 10,000 addresses.

Method
FastRoute trains a routing policy using proximal policy optimization (PPO)
against a traffic simulator calibrated on anonymized real delivery GPS
traces from a logistics partner. The reward function penalizes total
delivery time and lateness relative to promised delivery windows.

Results
Across 500 simulated delivery days, FastRoute reduced average delivery time
from 42 minutes to 31 minutes compared to the shortest-path baseline, a 26%
reduction. Late deliveries dropped from 12% to 7% of all deliveries.

Limitations
All evaluation was done in simulation calibrated on one city's traffic
patterns; real-world deployment may show different results, and we have not
yet tested FastRoute against live traffic conditions.
